❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Visa Run & Immigration Questions Answered

Everything you need to know before taking your visa run with Vietnam Visa Pass.

1. Which border gate should my nationality choose (Laos vs. Cambodia)? +

• Nha Trang ⇄ Moc Bai (Cambodia): Recommended for USA, UK, Australia, Ukraine, Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Brazil, EU... due to straightforward Cambodia visa-on-arrival procedures.
• Da Nang ⇄ Lao Bao (Laos): Lao Bao is an International Border Gate and DOES NOT restrict by nationality — it accepts and clears ALL global citizens.
• Nha Trang ⇄ Bo Y Border Gate (Laos): Recommended for citizens of Russia, South Korea, Belarus, Malaysia, Czech Republic... who enjoy direct visa-free entry into Laos.

2. How much cash should I prepare for border stamping fees? +

• Laos Borders (Bo Y / Lao Bao): Prepare 140,000 VND (VN Exit: 50k, Laos Entry: 20k, Laos Exit: 20k, VN Re-entry: 50k). Keep cash ready inside your passport.
• Cambodia Border (Moc Bai): Prepare 50k-150k VN border fee, 100k-150k motor shuttle to Cambodia counter, and ~$35 – $50 USD for Cambodia visa.

3. Are there surcharges for restrictive nationalities? +

Under Immigration Department regulations, special nationalities require dossier appraisal: +850,000 VND for citizens of Egypt, Algeria, Morocco, Tunisia, Sri Lanka, Mauritius; and +650,000 VND for citizens of Turkey.

4. How should I take photos of the exit stamp for urgent E-Visa? +

Immediately upon receiving the exit stamp, photograph your passport first page: capture all 4 corners, ensure high clarity, avoid lamp reflection/glare, and never cover text with fingers. Send photo to VVE chat for immediate e-visa submission.

5. How does the 100% Money-Back & Government Fee Refund Guarantee work? +

We guarantee a 100% refund of our service fee if your e-visa is rejected due to service error. In addition, if the service cannot be performed or executed, the full Government State Fee is also 100% refunded under Article 5 of our Service Agreement.
